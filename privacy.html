Privacy Policy

Effective 27 September 2026

Tanu is an app for learning Japanese from real videos. It is made and run by an independent developer based in Australia ("I", "me"). This policy explains what information Tanu collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. I handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

In short: Tanu collects only what it needs to run your account, remember your learning, and improve the app. I don't sell your information, and there are no ads. You can delete your account and its data from inside the app at any time.

1. Information Tanu collects

Information you give

  • Account details: your email address and password when you create an account (passwords are stored securely hashed by our account provider; I can't see them). If you sign in with Apple or Google, I receive the email address those services share.
  • Onboarding answers: the name you'd like Tanu to call you, your Japanese level, why you're learning, the topics you like, your daily goal, reminder time and how you heard about Tanu.
  • Feedback: anything you send through "Send feedback", with the app version and phone type so I can fix problems.

Information created as you use Tanu

  • Learning activity: clips you watch (and roughly how much of each), clips you like or save. This is used to choose clips that suit you and avoid repeats.
  • On your phone only: your saved words and flashcard progress, daily watching time and streak are stored on your device.
  • Usage analytics: which screens and features are used (for example, which onboarding step people stop at), with basic device details such as phone model, operating system, app version and approximate location derived from your IP address. This helps me understand what works and fix what doesn't.

Payments

Subscriptions are bought through Apple's App Store (or Google Play). I never see or store your card or payment details. I receive only whether your subscription or free trial is active.

2. How it's used

  • To create and run your account and keep your progress.
  • To choose clips for your level and interests.
  • To send the daily reminder you chose (scheduled on your phone; you can turn it off in Settings or in your phone's settings).
  • To provide your subscription.
  • To answer your feedback and support requests.
  • To understand how Tanu is used and improve it.

3. Services that help run Tanu

Tanu uses a few trusted providers, who process information only to provide their service:

  • Supabase: accounts and database (profile, likes, saves, watch history, feedback).
  • PostHog: usage analytics.
  • Apple / Google: app distribution, payments, and "Sign in with Apple/Google" if you use them.
  • RevenueCat: tracks whether your subscription is active.
  • YouTube (Google): the videos in Tanu play through YouTube's embedded player, and YouTube may collect information under Google's Privacy Policy.

These providers may store information on servers outside Australia, including in the United States. Where that happens I take reasonable steps to make sure they protect it in line with this policy.

I don't sell or rent your personal information, and I don't share it for advertising. I would only disclose it if required by law.

4. How long it's kept

Account information is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, your account, profile, likes, saves and watch history are deleted from Tanu's database, and the data stored on your phone is removed. Feedback you sent stays but is no longer linked to you. Analytics are kept in an aggregated form and deleted on the analytics provider's normal schedule.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete account, in the app.
  • Reminders: turn them off in Settings or in your phone's notification settings.
  • Access or correct your information: email me and I'll help.
  • If you live in the EU/UK or elsewhere with similar laws, you may also have rights to object to or restrict processing and to data portability. Contact me to use them.

If you're unhappy with how I've handled your information, please contact me first. In Australia you can also complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

6. Children

Tanu isn't directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact me and I'll delete it.

7. Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections, and each person's data in the database can only be read by that person's account. No system is perfectly secure, but I work to protect your information.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, I'll update the date above, and for important changes I'll let you know in the app.
